# Block Drop 2: Pixel Quest

Volume 2 of [Block Drop](https://ploug76.github.io/block-drop/) (Volume 1 is unchanged). A FIVE8NINE Studios game.

**Play:** https://ploug76.github.io/block-drop-vol-2/

- **Quest:** 5 animated worlds (Neon Arcade, Pixel Jungle, Deep Blue Grid, Lava Core, Cyber Space), 30 stages: line goals, dig out junk, free gems, survive rising junk, and a boss in every world (Glitchy, Vine Byte, Bit Shark, Magma Cube, The Glitch King). 3 stars per stage, saved progress, story intro and ending with SKIP.
- **Marathon**, **Sprint 40** and **Ultra 2:00** with saved bests.
- Modern rules: 7-bag, SRS rotation with wall kicks, hold, 5-piece preview, ghost, lock delay, T-spins, QUAD DROPs, combos, back-to-back and perfect clears.
- Juice: line-clear bursts, hard-drop trails, screen shake, announcer pops, original WebAudio music per world.
- **Touch, no on-screen D-pad:** drag to move (the piece follows your finger), tap to rotate (left edge rotates back), drag down to soft drop, flick down to hard drop, swipe up to hold. Keyboard and game controllers (USB/Bluetooth) fully supported, menus included.
- Works offline (PWA) and updates itself on open.
